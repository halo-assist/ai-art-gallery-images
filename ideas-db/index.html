Questions

What exactly is this?

During beta, the Painpoint Index is a one-time-purchase database of curated, sourced business ideas, sold as a download. This page is the preview + early-bird list while we confirm enough people want it. If we don’t, we won’t waste your inbox.

How were these ideas picked?

Automated scanning of public Reddit threads, then a manual curation pass — every published idea links to its source post. Nothing here is invented: if a complaint isn’t real, the idea doesn’t make the Index.

How much will the full Index cost?

Early-bird pricing will sit under $40 one-time and lock in at launch. The form above asks what you’d pay — the honest answer helps us decide whether to build it at all.

What do I get for joining the early-bird list?

First access when the full Index opens, the early-bird price, and occasional build-in-public updates. One or two emails, that’s it.

Is this AI-generated slop?

The scan is automated; the curation and writeups are human-reviewed, and every quote is verbatim with a public source link. Judge it yourself — the five examples are free.
