# YouTube upload guide — 10 Shorts (Idea Lab: youtube-shorts)

All 10 Shorts are rendered, QA-passed and ready to publish. The agent cannot log into a
Google account, so this last step is yours. Estimated time: 20–30 minutes for all 10.

## What to do

1. **Pick the channel.** Any Google account you control; a fresh channel keeps this test
   separate (e.g. project.halo.assistant@gmail.com if you want it 100% away from your personal account).
2. **Get the files.** Download the 10 MP4s from the links in the notification email, or unzip
   `youtube_shorts_upload_bundle.zip`.

3. **Upload each one** in YouTube Studio (studio.youtube.com → Create → Upload video):
   - Title + description: copy from the table below (descriptions include #shorts hashtags).
   - Audience: *No, it's not made for kids*.
   - **Altered content: Yes** — 'this video contains altered or synthetic content' (the narration
     voice is synthetic). This is required by YouTube policy for realistic synthetic media.
   - Visibility: **Public**.
4. **Publish all 10.** All at once is fine; 3–4 per day also works if you prefer a gentler pace
   for a brand-new channel. Either way the observation window is the same.
5. **Reply** with the channel URL and confirm the 10 Shorts are live.

What happens next: the pipeline watches the channel's public stats for 30 days and emails you
a keep/kill verdict. Kill rule: median <300 views per Short once the 10th has been live ≥7 days.
